Lane Cues

Drop a coach's voice clips into your own swim playlist. The music dips under each cue, then swells back. You get one MP3 to copy onto open-ear or bone-conduction swim headphones. Everything stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

      Getting the MP3 onto swim headphones
      1. Connect the headphones to a computer with their charging/USB cable. Most MP3-mode swim headphones, such as the Shokz OpenSwim range, appear as a drive.
      2. Drag the saved MP3 onto that drive, then eject it before unplugging.
      3. Switch the headphones to MP3 mode if they also have Bluetooth.
      Works with MP3, M4A (AAC) and WAV. Music bought from or downloaded through Apple Music is copy-protected and can't be read.
      MP3 encoding uses lamejs, a port of LAME (LGPL).
      Keep a mix to about an hour, especially on a phone: the browser holds every track in memory while mixing.
